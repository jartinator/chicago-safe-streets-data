To: Freedom of Information Officer, Office of the City Clerk
Re: FOIA request under the Illinois Freedom of Information Act, 5 ILCS 140

Dear FOIA Officer,

Under the Illinois Freedom of Information Act (5 ILCS 140), I request the
following records, electronically where they exist in electronic form:

1. Presentations, reports, or written testimony submitted by the Chicago
   Department of Transportation to the Committee on Pedestrian and Traffic
   Safety or the Committee on Transportation and Public Way that report
   bikeway or bike-lane mileage (total miles, miles installed, or miles by
   facility type), from January 1, 2015 to the present.

2. Committee meeting minutes or records of proceedings for the same
   committees and period, to the extent they record bikeway-mileage figures
   presented by CDOT.

3. Any annual report from CDOT to the City Council for the same period
   containing bikeway or bike-lane mileage figures.

I request these for a noncommercial public-interest purpose — an open,
public dashboard analyzing bicycle-infrastructure trends in Chicago — and
request a waiver or reduction of fees under 5 ILCS 140/6. If any part of
this request is unduly burdensome under 5 ILCS 140/3(g), please contact me
so I can narrow it. Electronic delivery is preferred.
